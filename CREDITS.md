The game rescales the airframe, adjusts its canopy material, and adds its own weapon mounts.

Converted from AC3D to a scaled GLB with revised materials, separate rotor pivots, trimmed cockpit detail, and replacement stores. The [source archive and license](public/assets/models/apache-source.zip) are also downloadable from the game briefing, including offline.
